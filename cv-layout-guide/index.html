CV Layout and Wording: What a South African CV Should Look Like

Most people know what to put on a CV but struggle with how to word it. This guide covers the layout and gives you wording you can adapt.

The standard layout

  1. Name and contact details
  2. Summary
  3. Work experience
  4. Education
  5. Skills
  6. References

Use one clean font, clear headings and plenty of white space. Avoid tables and graphics, which can confuse the software many employers use to scan CVs.

Sample summary lines

  • School leaver: Reliable matric graduate with strong computer skills and community volunteering experience, seeking an entry-level administration role.
  • Graduate: Recent B.Com graduate with internship experience in accounts payable, looking to start a career in finance.
  • Experienced worker: Warehouse supervisor with six years of experience managing a team of ten and improving stock accuracy.

Turning duties into achievements

  • Instead of "Responsible for cashier duties", write "Processed 150+ transactions daily with accurate till balances".
  • Instead of "Helped customers", write "Resolved customer queries in person and by phone, keeping waiting times short".
  • Instead of "Did filing", write "Organised and maintained records for 300+ client files".

Only use numbers you can back up if an interviewer asks.
