How to Find Government Jobs in South Africa: Where to Look and How to Apply

Public service jobs attract thousands of applicants because they offer stability and clear career paths. They also follow stricter rules than most private employers, so knowing the process matters.

Where government vacancies are advertised

  • The Public Service Vacancy Circular, published regularly for national and provincial departments
  • Department and municipality websites, which often post their own notices
  • Job boards that collect public sector adverts in one place, such as government jobs in South Africa listed by province

Read the advert before anything else

Every advert states the post reference number, the closing date, the minimum requirements and where to send your application. Departments differ on these details, so don't assume last time's process still applies.

Documents to prepare

  • A completed Z83 application form (see the Z83 walkthrough on this site or the full Z83 guide)
  • An up-to-date CV with referees
  • Copies of your ID and qualifications, in the format the advert asks for
  • A driver's licence copy if the post requires one

Mistakes that get applications discarded

  1. Applying after the closing date
  2. Leaving out the post reference number
  3. Sending documents the advert didn't ask for, or missing ones it did
  4. Using a generic CV that doesn't match the post's requirements

A simple weekly routine

Check new adverts twice a week, keep your documents in one folder, and keep a note of every post you applied for with its closing date. Many departments don't contact unsuccessful applicants, so tracking your own applications saves confusion.
