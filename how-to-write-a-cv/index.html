How to Write a CV in South Africa: A Step-by-Step Guide

A good CV is short, clear and matched to the job. Follow these steps and you'll have a solid first draft in under an hour.

Step 1: Contact details

Full name, phone number, a professional email address and your town or city. Employers often filter by area, so include it. You don't need a photo.

Step 2: A two- or three-line summary

Say who you are, what you've done and what you're looking for. Keep it specific: "Matric graduate with 12 months of retail experience, looking for a customer service role" works better than a list of personality words.

Step 3: Work experience

List jobs from most recent to oldest. For each one, give the employer, your title, the dates and two to four bullet points showing what you achieved, not just what you were responsible for.

Step 4: Education and training

Start with your highest qualification. Include short courses if they relate to the job.

Step 5: Skills

Keep this list to skills you can actually demonstrate, such as MS Excel, customer service, driving or a second language.

Step 6: References

Two or three people who have worked with you and have agreed to be contacted.

Step 7: Check and save

Keep it to two pages, check spelling, and save it as a PDF so the layout doesn't change when the employer opens it.

Need a starting point?

If you'd rather not begin from a blank page, you can build your CV online or look through CV examples to see how others set theirs out. When it's ready, browse the latest South African job vacancies and start applying.
